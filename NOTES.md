Kingside 1.0.1: the computer opponents retuned.

- Every level was measured in games against a reference chess engine and adjusted, so each one now plays at the rating it shows, about 200 apart. Several levels were weaker or stronger than their rating said.
- Eleanor is now shown as about 2500. She plays exactly as before.
- Your rating from rated games is worked out against these ratings, so it is now more accurate.
