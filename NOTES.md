First release of Kingside: chess for Android that never goes online.

- Ten computer opponents with names and faces, from Alfie (about 400) to Eleanor (about 2400), and Robin, who plays at your level
- Four 3D piece sets and a flat one, five boards, animations and sounds
- Pass the phone, or link two phones with no internet: the same Wi-Fi, a hotspot, a direct Wi-Fi link or Bluetooth
- 50,000 checkmate puzzles, 28 lessons, and a review of every finished game
- Extras you can switch on: chess clocks, rated games, opening names, Chess960, a board editor and achievements
- No adverts, no account, nothing to buy

**To install:** download `Kingside.apk`, open it, and allow your browser or Files app to install it if Android asks. Android 8 or later.
