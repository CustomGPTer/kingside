Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. This policy covers the Kingside app for Android (package uk.jj.kingside) and this website. Kingside is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Your games, stats, rating and settings are kept on your phone. They are never uploaded to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Kingside never contacts the internet.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • When two phones are linked, they send each other only what the game needs.

What stays on your phone

Kingside keeps these in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Your games: the moves, the result, clock times and any review.
  • Your stats and rating, the puzzles you have solved and the lessons you have finished.
  • Your settings, and the name you choose for two-phone games.

Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for Kingside, so none of this is copied to a Google account.

Playing on two phones

When you host or join a game, the two phones send each other only what the game needs: the name each player typed in, the starting position, the colours, the clock, the moves, and requests for a draw, a take-back or a rematch. This goes straight from one phone to the other over your own Wi-Fi, a hotspot, a direct Wi-Fi link or Bluetooth. It does not pass through the developer or any server.

Sharing games and the app

A game is shared or copied only when you ask, as plain text that goes to the app you choose. Send Kingside to a friend hands over the app file itself, by your phone's share list or from your phone to theirs over local Wi-Fi. The links to this website in Settings open in your browser, which is covered by the browser's own policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can remove any game from the Games list, and uninstalling Kingside deletes everything the app has kept.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the page itself. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Children

Kingside collects nothing from anyone, so it is suitable for all ages. Younger players should ask a parent before linking phones with people they don't know.

Contact
