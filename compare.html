What Kingside does not do

Kingside has no online play over the internet and no matching with strangers: the two phones need to be on the same Wi-Fi or close together. It has no videos or coaches, and its ratings are estimates.

Where the others do more

Chess - Play and Learn matches you with players anywhere in the world and has a large library of lessons and videos. Both update themselves from Google Play.
