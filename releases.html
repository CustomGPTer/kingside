Release notes

What changed in each version of Kingside. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 22 November 2026Latest

  • First release. Chess with every rule: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate, repetition, the fifty-move rule and dead positions.
  • Ten computer opponents with names and faces, from Alfie (about 400) to Eleanor (about 2400), and Robin, who plays at your level.
  • Four 3D piece sets and a flat one, five boards, move and capture animations, original sounds and optional vibration.
  • Hints that explain themselves, undo, and a plain reason when a move is not allowed.
  • Pass the phone for two players, with three board layouts.
  • Two phones with no internet: the same Wi-Fi, a hotspot, a direct Wi-Fi link or Bluetooth, with draw offers, take-backs, rematches and a game that carries on after a dropped link.
  • Send Kingside to a friend by Quick Share or Bluetooth, or from your phone to theirs over Wi-Fi.
  • 50,000 checkmate puzzles from mate in one to mate in five, a daily puzzle, a streak and a three-minute rush.
  • 28 lessons, from how the pieces move to endgames.
  • Every game kept, with review: a graph of who was winning, accuracy, and a label on every move.
  • Stats, and bringing games in and out as PGN.
  • Extras that are off until you switch them on: chess clocks, rated games, opening names, Chess960, a board editor and achievements.
  • Colour-blind friendly highlights, larger text, and moves in words with screen reader support.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called Kingside.apk. It is 5.0 MB (5,013,735 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

d5cdf45be9f156d1695d6311cc117283185609115cc90e609d52d5521a21e1b1

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Kingside.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
